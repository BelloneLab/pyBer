# pyBer 0.58.0

Align a photometry trace to an injection, with or without a behavior file.

## Use injection times

1. Open **Setup → Recordings and groups** and enable **Injection time**.
2. Click a recording’s entry and enter hours, minutes and seconds. Choose elapsed time from the first loaded photometry sample, or enter the time on the synchronized video clock. Leave a recording at **None** if it has no injection. An entry of **00:00:00** means the injection occurred at the chosen clock’s zero.
3. Set **Align source** to **Injection**. Each included recording contributes one event. Negative times precede injection and positive times follow it.

For scored behavior, choose **Behavior** as the alignment source and select **Before injection** or **After injection**. **Compare before and after** shows onset counts, observed seconds, onsets per minute and time spent in behavior. A bout spanning injection contributes its time to both periods. An onset exactly at injection belongs to the after period.

Project files save injection times per recording. Older projects open with **None** for recordings without an injection entry. The postprocessing export manifest records the entered time, clock and available photometry and camera timestamps.

## Downloads

- **pyBer-windows.exe** runs the Windows desktop app.
- **pyBer-cli-windows.exe** runs the Windows command-line tools.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** lists the checksums for both executables.
